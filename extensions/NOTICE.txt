Modifications in this build, made for the Runaldinho deployment at
https://juicebox444.github.io/runaldinho/:
  - the SPA origin https://juicebox444.github.io/* was added to
    content_scripts.matches and externally_connectable.matches;
  - the bridge-core sender allowlist accepts that origin;
  - a failed Garmin call carries Garmin's own refusal reason (the response
    body, trimmed) in the error message instead of only the status code;
  - nothing else was changed.

The extensions are provided as unpacked builds for Chromium browsers. They
are not published on the Chrome Web Store by the Runaldinho author.
